What to expect on day one

Story mode: that's the goal. Decanter runs Windows games on Apple silicon with Wine and Apple's D3DMetal, which turns DirectX 12 into Metal. GTA V's story mode already runs on Mac this way, and GTA 6 on PC will very likely use DirectX 12 too. We'll say "it works" only after we've played it.

GTA 6 Online: very likely not. GTA Online on PC uses BattlEye anti-cheat, which needs Windows itself and doesn't run on Mac or Linux. Expect GTA 6 Online to work the same way. No Mac app can get around that, and we won't pretend.

Your Mac: Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. GTA 6 is heavy: Pro, Max and Ultra chips will do best, and Decanter's Performance setting, frame-rate limit and MetalFX upscaling help everywhere else.
