# Eclipse Realms — the campaign plan

Kept by the Dungeon Master. Started 2026-10-22. Read with `docs/world/canon.md`, which holds the truth; this file holds
the plan. It covers the story from the Prologue to the Realms, the side lines that hang off it, where every clue is
planted and where it pays off, and the builders' queue.

**DM rules for every quest here.** Each quest has:
- a hook;
- clues in the world: a readable thing, an odd tile, a rumour, something that happens only at night;
- a puzzle or a choice;
- a boss with a reason to exist;
- a unique with its own look and lore;
- consequences that other NPCs remember.

**Pattern note.** The first three shipped chains all end in "give it back or keep it". Their twists also share a
shape: a thin ghost, and a choir or a smith who walked into the eclipse. That is three in a row. **The next chains must
vary both the choice and the twist.** In the queue below:
- #1 ends on *who you tell*.
- #2 ends on *what happens to a city's heat*.
- #3 ends on *whether a village keeps a light for strangers*.

None of their twists is "a thin ghost".

## 1. The main arc

Each land is an act (design-bible.md §3.3, realms-endgame.md §2). An act has:
- a **gate chain** (D2-shaped), given in the act town;
- an **act boss** at the bottom of the deepest dungeon;
- a **Seal**, which is the chain's completion fact;
- one **Witness chapter**: Margin Notes in one Keeper's hand;
- one **fresh tally mark** in the act boss's room;
- a **world change** that NPCs remember.

Sea routes are gated by the act boss (`play/harbour/harbour.i18n.ts:43-45`). Realms are gated by Seals.

### Prologue: "The Fresh Mark" (Ember Isle, 1–10)
- **Dread.** The Hushed Barrow's graves will not stay closed, and the Eclipse Beacon's light leans *down* the mine
  shaft instead of out to sea.
- **Beats.**
  1. Glim finds the Keeper at Ember Harbour.
  2. The first hearth (built; `td-prologue`).
  3. ***The Sexton's Count*** (built, level 6). The Keeper learns that the dead here are *uncounted*, not evil.
  4. ***The Beacon Goes Down*** (queue #1, level 8). It is the gate chain:
     - the beacon leans;
     - the Ninefold Stones carry the five tally marks;
     - the Ember Warden falls;
     - a **sixth mark, fresh** waits behind the Warden's ring.
- **Act boss.** The Ember Warden (Ember Mine −3).
- **Seal.** The *Seamworks Tally*.
- **World change.** The beacon shines out to sea again. Odd Tarn says ships see it from Pyrehold. Kael looks up from
  the anvil for the first time.

### Act I: "What Drinks the Heat" (Infernal Reach, 8–30)
- **Dread.** Pyrehold's great forges are cold at the bottom. The caldera smoke leans "like it's listening".
- **Beats.**
  1. Town tasks (built).
  2. ***The Smith Who Forgot His Own Hammer*** ([Q2], 12–18). The Keeper meets their first Wanewalker.
  3. The Ashen Crypt and the Ossuary Watch: an oath kept past death.
  4. ***What Drinks the Heat*** (queue #2, 18–30), the gate chain:
     - frost-glass in the Glass Halls;
     - cold anvils in the Glass Foundry;
     - a riddle in Cant (*ul mur*: "the seam, walled");
     - the Cinder Sovereign.
- **Act boss.** The Cinder Sovereign (Molten Deep −6).
- **Seal.** The *Cinder Seal*.
- **Witness.** Ash's chapter, *The Price of a Seam*.
- **World change.** It depends on the choice: Pyrehold's forges roar, or stay banked.
  - Either way the seam breathes harder at the next Infernal-leaning eclipse.
  - Odette starts forecasting.
  - The ship to the Verdant opens.

### Act II: "The Wood Wears a Lantern" (Verdant Wilds, 15–40)
- **Dread.** The forest regrows *wrong*: copied trees, and a tree that wears a lantern.
- **Beats.**
  1. Town tasks (built).
  2. ***The Hollow Choir*** ([Q2], 22–30). The Keeper learns why the wood used to stay true.
  3. The Root Hollows.
  4. The gate chain, ***The Wood Wears a Lantern*** (queue #7):
     - Ilsa Fenn finds the copy-tree;
     - the Mossbound Colossus;
     - the Elder Rootwyrm;
     - a sealed root-door in the Rootcradle opens on **the Rootcradle Below**.
- **Act boss.** The **Graft Crown**, a copy that wants to be the original (Rootcradle Below, ~45).
- **Seal.** The *Thorn Seal*.
- **Witness.** Thorn.
- **World change.** It depends on the Choir's choice:
  - *freed*: the copies die back slowly, and the wood grows a little crooked but its own;
  - *sung*: the wood is true but the bells still ring.

  Ilsa remembers either way.

### Act III: "Who Carries the Second Lantern" (Umbral Marches, 26–50)
- **Dread.** The fog thins round lanterns, and round *something else* that carries one.
- **Beats.**
  1. The first Umbral keepers (queue #6).
  2. ***The Tenth Lantern*** (queue #3, 30–38). It plants the Lamplighter: "For whoever comes next".
  3. The Drowned Crypts and the Ink Abbess.
  4. The gate chain, ***Who Carries the Second Lantern***:
     - **the Lamplighter Below** is met at last, as a sixth Keeper's apprentice;
     - Veil's twist: the Mirror Parliament is the four signers' consensus, copied, still voting.
- **Act boss.** The Mirror Parliament (the Ink Deeps, below Drowned Crypts −6).
- **Seal.** The *Veil Seal*.
- **World change.** The Lamplighter visits the Homestead. The Rejuvenations get a name.

### Act IV: "The Charts Are Right" (Celestial Peaks, 35–60), unbuilt
- **Dread.** The stars are in the wrong places on the old charts, or the charts are right.
- **Cast.** Sera Quill and Nox Aster (the struck column); the Archivist publishes the struck roll.
- **Bosses.** The Frostfang Matriarch, then **the Perihelion Engine** (the Orrery Vault).
- **Seal.** *Drift*.

### Act V: "Noon Without End" (Luminous Expanse, 45–70), unbuilt
- **Dread.** Shadows that point the wrong way.
- **Cast.** Aureth: "The Sun did not break. The Sun let go."
- **Bosses.** The Mirror Labyrinth's Echo, then **the Pale Refrain** (the Sunken Palace −6).
- **Seal.** *Glint*.
- **Payoff.** The truth is stated for the first time: the eclipse is the seam breathing.

### Finale: "The Sixth Mark" (the Godforge threshold under the changed Seamworks, 70+)
- The Keeper brings the five Seals to Kael. When the fifth is laid on it, **the anvil rings on its own**, and Kael tells
  what happened to his eye.
- The Keeper goes down through the five sockets to **the Unbound Pattern**. Its phase-2 title is *the Unanimous*.
- **The choice.** Carve the sixth mark, or leave it blank. This sets the Seam Stance, *Carved* or *Blank*.
- Then **the Unbound Seal** opens the Realms and Hell-tier.

before building.

`after` ordering and one typed riddle. Two small extensions are asked for below. Each is marked **(framework +)**.

### #4 [spec'd]: Zone atmosphere pass (owner direction 2026-10-22: a dark D&D feel in every zone)

**New objects** (`td-level.ts` `MapObject` plus sprites in `td-art.ts`; cozy pixel style, outlined `#140c1c`):
- `altar`: a stone block with a dry cloth.
- `brazier`: unlit. `brazier-lit` is a fixed light, r4.
- `cage`
- `chains`: a wall hang, no block.
- `statue-broken`: blocks.
- `cobweb`: a corner decal, no block.
- `circle`: a floor decal, salt or chalk.
- `inscription`: a readable wall tile. It is a station with an i18n line `zone.<map>.<n>`, EN and ES.
- `stain`: a dry rust-brown floor decal. Never red; never fresh.
- `rime`: a frost decal.

**Rules.**
- Torches stay only at stairs (the safe-arrival rule), at boss rings, and where a story keeps them lit.
- Each removed torch is replaced by darkness, by the lair's own glow, or by an **unlit brazier** the Keeper may light.
- Every floor gains **one inscription**, **one sealed or hidden thing** and **one ambient sound**.

The three zones that need it most, by the numbers (torches per floor; bones; nothing readable):

**(a) The Molten Deep (−1…−6)**: 8–12 torches a floor, 0–1 bones, no inscriptions. It is the Act I spine, and #2 needs
its clues.

  - −1: "Turn back at the second pool. — the Watch".
  - −2: "Cold on the side that faces down".
  - −4: "Bridge rebuilt: Year 41. Year 88. Year 130." (counted years, all under the current count).
  - −5: the pit riddle.
  - −6: the tally.

**(b) The Overgrown Temple (−1…−5)**: 6–8 torches a floor in a temple three centuries dead. The Hollow Choir [Q2]

  - −1: remove all torches except the stair. Keep **two shafts of daylight** (a light decal) through the vinewall,
