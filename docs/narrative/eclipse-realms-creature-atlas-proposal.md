# Eclipse Realms — Creature Atlas & Design Map (Proposal)

**Status:** Design-ready narrative proposal; no creature concept art is approved or created by this document.

## Purpose and source boundary

This atlas maps each proposed creature to a realm, landmark, and story function so that a future concept artist can create a coherent bestiary rather than generic enemies. It is grounded in the existing realm-scene masters in `Assets/Locations/` and the approved material language in the lore and art-direction canon.

The asset library currently contains no approved creature, enemy, or boss master. Names, personalities, and designs below are non-canon until owner approval. They are briefs for future creature concepts, not runtime requirements, combat specifications, or asset requests.

## Shared creature rules

- A creature must reveal a realm’s unresolved conflict, not merely provide combat.
- “Personality” means an observable drive and response to the Keeper; it does not require human speech.
- The Unbound Pattern corrupts by narrowing alternatives. Corrupted creatures should look like their realm’s function repeated past consent, not like a separate evil faction.
- Boss-scale threats are place-sized crises. They need readable vulnerability, consequence, and evidence—not arbitrary hit points or a generic monster silhouette.
- Future art must use two or more approved materials: carved metal, fractured crystal, obsidian, runic light, ancient masonry, living rootwork, smoky void, or stardust.

## Art-production handoff

Before a creature enters production, approve its lore entry and create a single 3:4 or 4:5 creature concept sheet. The sheet should show front silhouette, material close-ups, scale against the neutral Keeper, one behavior pose, and one corruption/escalation cue. Create transparent implementation renders only after the concept is approved. Bosses also require a landmark-overlap composition proving that their scale does not obscure the chapter’s inspectable evidence.
