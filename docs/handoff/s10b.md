# S10b: the Last Siege and the Fogmother (branch `s10b`, 2026-11-04)

Campaign S10 (the rest of it); design `docs/design/monsters-abilities.md` §6.2, §6.4. Base: 1be211e2 (main + S10's
Antlered Storm, docs/handoff/s10boss.md). Everything reuses S10: td-party-boss.ts, threat, room-world `weatherBoss`,
the `mech` event, td-party-fx's rings, combat-sim's `--storm` cells.

## What a player gets
- **The Last Siege** (32), the Ashen Bastion's siege tower, on an **ashfall night** in the Pyre Peaks (room area
  `infernal-reach-pyre-peaks`): it rolls out of **the Siege Trench** (tile 110,40) up the Bastion Road on a fixed track
  (`WorldBossDef.track`, six points to the Bastion's milestone), a tile every 4 s, one a second on its Last Turn. It
  never turns or chases; it bites its fixated anywhere in its ten-tile arena (the ram, the crossbows). It stands for the
  rest of the ash's window; at the track's end it leaves unfelled ("The war is over. It knows now.", omen `home`).
  3 h cooldown. Drawn on its own body (`look: 'last-siege'`: ash-grey timber, iron bands, a ram, two dead watchmen at
  the slits, the catapult's coal, the Bastion's torn banner, turning wheels), ×3 HD / ×2 flat, with embers off the coal.
  - 100–65%: Fixate + Sunder, **the Garrison Rides** (`osk brann sha`): three crossbow rings r1 round the top threat.
  - 65–30%: the volley, **Load the Catapult** (`brann vael dra`): three meteor rings r2.5 on the healer's side, 2.5B
    fire; the Add wave down the ladders every 20 s (Bone Walkers and Bone Priests, whose Muster is theirs to interrupt).
  - 30–0%: **the Last Turn** (it speeds up), Burn the Room in fire every 15 s, a Soak at the ram every 12 s.
  - **The Wall That Kept Walking** (shield, Legendary only, 1 in 10): block, Defense, fire resist, life (shields roll no attack damage: §6.2's +12% is left off); still
    for 2 s, 15% less damage for him (td-mobs) and for party members within 3 tiles (an `aura` ally claim the room
    carries, ally-claims ALLY_RELICS / CUT_AURAS). Mats: cinder ore ×4, infernal heartstone ×2.
- **The Fogmother** (54) at **the Three Posts** (the Black Meres' widest pool, tile -4,-12; firm mire and reed islands to
  11 tiles, three dark lantern posts at its edge): on a **foggy night** she gathers a minute and stands. A Keeper
  standing 3 s at each post lights it; all three lit before she stands and she keeps down until dawn (omen `kept`).
  4 h cooldown. Her own body (`look: 'fogmother'`: tall grey habits, a veil, two cold points, no feet, water beading),
  fog wisps circling her hem. §6.4's own Lightless aura is left off: at night in fog it blacked the screen and the dark took a low Keeper; the Settle carries the light.
  - 100–65%: Fixate, **Wrap** (`vor nyr mur`): a grey line to her fixated, 0.5B cold + Chill 3 s.
  - 65–30%: Wrap, **She Sings Your Name** (`nyr sha`): Brand on the healer and one other; **Gloam Blizzard**
    (`rim vor dra`): rings r3 over the two farthest, Frost (a Wet Keeper freezes); the Drowned Knights and Acolytes
    every 20 s; the acolytes' **Call the Watch** (`osk brann teth`) channel summons an elite knight pack (interrupt).
  - 30–0%: **Settle** (`nyr orum`): every screen veils the floor but three tiles round its Keeper and each lit post's
    r4 light; Burn the Room in cold, a Soak on the tank. All three posts lit in this phase: she deals 25% less.
    A lit post is a light she will not stand in (td-party-boss `outOfLight`).
  - **The Psalm Heard Through Water** (amulet slot `trinket`, Legendary only, 1 in 10): cold and lightning resist, mana,
    cast speed; 25% more healing; a heal on a Keeper in fog or darkness lifts Chill and lends +1 light 4 s (self in
    td-mobs; an ally through a relic `cleanse` claim with `f` PSALM_F, Chill only). Mats: umbral ink ×5, nightglass ×3,
    inkstone ×2.
- Glim's lines on rising; rumours on those nights (td-sky-lines `night: true`): Captain Vey and Garrick Dross (ashfall),
  Old Corvane and Dace Morrow (fog). EN + ES. Lore: docs/world/lore-addendum.md; canon §5 rows updated.

## Director lessons applied up front
- World bosses ×3 HD / ×2 flat, integer nearest-neighbour, each with its own body and a small identity mark (embers /
  wisps), steady on Low and reduced motion.
- Every fatal telegraph (volley, meteor, blizzard, burn, the heavy) is a `pbhit` ring: saturated red-orange, 2-px dark
  outline, growing fill; the soak gold. Wrap's line and the posts' light are drawn with the rings in `drawPartyOver`,
  above the weather and the plates; the post's flame upright above its glass. Settle is two hard-edged flat fills.
