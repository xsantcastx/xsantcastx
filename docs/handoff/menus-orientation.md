Owner, 2026-10-15: "the menus need to be better when playing horizontal or vertical … we need the menus to be
perfect", plus a real-phone bug report (`docs/references/owner-ui/bug-satchel-{landscape,portrait}.jpg`: the

(`docs/references/owner-ui/maplestory-equip-inventory-stats.webp`: window chrome with a title bar and ×, the doll
framed by wells, category tabs over a slot grid, the currency row at the bottom).

Shots: `docs/handoff/menus-orientation/before-<menu>-<size>.png` (base `7faf5375`) and `after-…` (this branch).
The full grid (every menu × 390×844, 844×390, 932×430, 360×740, 820×1180, 1180×820, 1280×800) is regenerated by

## The system

Every in-world menu is one window: a **title bar** (name, optional faces/tabs) with **one 44 px × close** at the
top-right, a body that scrolls, at most one primary action. Where it sits depends only on how the phone is held —
pure CSS in the last block of `play/side-scroller.component.css` ("Menus: one window system"), so turning the phone
re-flows the same DOM: scroll position, the picked pocket and the open card survive the turn.

| Held | Where the window goes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait phone (≤900 px wide, upright) | **Bottom sheet**: full width (≤640 px, centred on a tablet), 85 % tall, rounded top, a **grip** on its title bar — pull it down >80 px to put it away (`play/sheet-drag.directive.ts`), padding clears the home indicator (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`). The bottom controls hide while it is up (`.stage--menu`). | Thumb reach; the top band (area, health, the satchel button) stays readable above it. |
| Landscape phone (≤900 px wide sideways, or a short touch screen ≤500 px tall such as 932×430) | **Side panel** on the right, full height, ≤60 % wide (Satchel 66 %, small menus 48 %), notch/home-bar safe. | The world and the move stick stay in view on the left; nothing sits under the left thumb. |
| Desktop / tablet landscape | A window where each one always stood. | Unchanged layout, same chrome. |

Frames that follow it: `.book` (Satchel), `.places` (Menu), `.place` (every station, the Slate's container, the
Glass of Becoming, Character, Codex, Skills, Waystone), `.anchor-sheet` (Rift Anchors), `.win-host` (Sound, Pets).
All sit at z 7–8 over the HUD bands, so no control ever lies on top of a menu (the old Satchel was z 4, under the
touch row — that was the owner's "not clickable"). The story card and Glim's talk stay centred dialogs; their veil
now pads for the safe areas.

**Stations inside a side panel.** The station kit queried the *viewport* (`@media (max-width: 640px)`), so a
sideways phone (844 px wide) got the desktop list | detail split squeezed into a 500 px panel. The shell is now an
inline-size container (`container: station`), and the journal, its list, the requirement grid and the shell have a
matching `@container station (…)` rule beside each `@media` one. Short screens (`max-height: 500px`) trim the shell
and skill-strip padding and fold the strip's hint line.

**One primary action in reach.** Inside the world's panel the station kit's primary `app-action-button` is
`position: sticky; bottom: 0` (via `--ab-stick`, set only by `.place-body`, so full pages are unchanged): Craft,
Sell, Buy, Strike, Spin ride the panel's bottom edge while their card is in view.

## The Satchel (MapleStory window, Diablo II doll)

- Title bar: **Satchel · [Worn | Stats] · ×**. Stats is the doll's other face (the live stat sheet), not a fold.
- One scroller (`.book-body`). Portrait: doll, then the bag. Landscape: two columns — the doll on the left (sticky,
  its cell size fitted to the height so all five rows show), the bag on the right.
- **Bag tabs** over the grid, only the ones with something in them: All · Gear · Charms · Materials.
- **Purse row** at the bag's bottom (sticky): Gold, then `n / cap slots` with its bar (`.capacity span` kept).
- **The item card** is in flow under the body upright (≤48 %, its own scroll, the action row sticky inside it), and
  over the bag column sideways (the doll — what it compares against — stays in view). Equip / Stow / Open are 44 px.
- **Empty wells show a faint pixel picture** of what goes there (sword, shield, helm, ring, coin…; `WELL_GHOST`),
  never a word that spills out of the box; the name is the `title` and `aria-label`.

## Icons: no more initials

Charms are minted (`charm-students-thread-<time>-<n>`) and stored with a `charm:<name>` definition, so `itemArt()`
found nothing and the bag drew "ST". Now: 15 charm icons in `item-icons.ts` (find = luck, fortune = coins and
marks, insight = thread, ink and glass), and `itemArt()` resolves a minted id's seed prefix and, last, the
display name (every `ITEM_DEFINITIONS` item and charm). The Exchange's equipment rows also carry their icon now
(they were "U" for Uncommon). Pinned by `item-icons.spec.ts` ("gives every charm its pixel icon, minted ids
included").

## Audit — before → after

Measured by the spec's measure mode over 24 menus at each size (the owner's satchel: materials, a dozen charms, one
worn). "Primary covered/off-screen" counts a primary that a hit test misses even after scrolling it into view;
"covered" counts controls in plain view something else lies on top of; "sub-44" counts every smaller control
inside the menus (mostly hosted pages' own chips, tracked, not all fixed).

| size | before: no 44px × · close covered · primary missed · cut off · covered · covers move stick | after |

| 390×844 | 2 · 0 · 2 · 0 · 0 · — | 0 · 0 · 0 · 0 · 0 · — |

| 932×430 | 2 · **2** · **6** · **1** · **2** · **19** | 0 · 0 · 0 · 0 · 0 · 0 |

¹ After a probe fix: closed `<details>` bodies report rects in Chrome and were counted as covered.

Defects found (before) and what fixed each:
- **Satchel sideways** (owner photo): Legs, Feet and Pets wells cut off by the book's `overflow: hidden`; the grid
  not reachable; the item card's × under the touch row (the book was z 4, the touch row z 4 and later in the DOM);
  Equip 32 px tall. → side panel, two columns, sticky doll, card over the bag column, z 7.
- **Satchel upright** (owner photo): the grid squeezed into a 1½-row scroller under the doll, the card covering
  it with Equip cut off at the panel's edge. → one scroller, the card in flow with its action row sticky.
- **Charms as initials** ("SC", "ST", "CK"). → charm icons + `itemArt` minted/name fallback; Exchange rows too.
- **Well labels spilling out** ("TRINKET", "OFF-HAND" in 8 px caps). → ghost pictures.
- **Every station sideways** was a full-screen sheet over the move stick (20 of 24 menus at 844×390) with its
  primary below the fold in 7. → side panel + the kit's container queries + sticky primary.
- **Places menu** had no close at all (toggle only). → title bar and ×.
- **Sound / Pets**: no 44 px square close ("Done"/"Close" words, 64–69 px wide), Pets' close under the Satchel,
  the Sound sheet's last lines below the viewport at 844×390. → host frames, scroll, × close, z 8.
- **Desktop**: station/place closes were 75×36 text buttons ("CLOSE Esc"). → the 44 px × (Esc still works; it is
  the `title` and `aria-keyshortcuts`).

## Tests

`e2e/play-menus-orientation.spec.ts` (chromium, phone context: touch + mobile):
- at **844×390, 390×844 and 360×740**, all 26 menus: opens; nothing outside the viewport; no sideways scroll; the
  close is ≥44 px and hit-testable; the primary (where present) is ≥44 px and hit-testable; no control cut by a box
  that does not scroll; no control in plain view covered by anything (a control under the sticky primary bar must
  scroll out from under it); in landscape the move stick is uncovered; the close closes it;
- the Satchel at each size: `elementFromPoint` on **every well, every pocket and every card button**, no empty
  well spelt as a word, no pocket drawn as initials, and Equip from a charm's card fills a charm well;
- **turning the phone mid-menu** (Satchel with a charm card open, the Crafting Bench, the Menu): portrait →
  landscape → portrait keeps it open, inside the viewport, closable, its primary reachable and its scroll position.

Changed selectors: the Satchel's "Stats" is `.book-more` inside `.book-faces` (still `aria-controls="satchel-stats"`);
closes are × glyphs with the old words as the accessible name (`Close`, `Done`), so `getByRole('button', { name })`
specs are unaffected. The Menu got a title bar (`.places-close`); focus on open still goes to the first place row.
