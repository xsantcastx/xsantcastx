# Forge Thralls (I1 pixel icons)

Folder: `Assets/Market/Items/Thralls/`. Pixel scale, palettes and the export rules are in the art bible, [docs/art/style-guide.md](../../art/style-guide.md); the save checklist is in [../README.md](../README.md). Paste **Prompt** as the positive prompt and **Negative** as the negative prompt, then clean the result on the art-pixel grid (style guide §14). The spec line is the export contract.

The five tiers read as one figure family that grows in gear and bearing; the tier colour is the shelf row's rim, not a glow.
