# Realm town key art (paintings)

Folder: `Assets/Locations/Towns/`. These stay **paintings** (style guide §1): the painterly style block and the S-specs are in [../README.md](../README.md). Paste **Prompt** as the positive prompt and **Negative** as the negative prompt; the spec line is the export contract.

**Demoted to key art (2026-10-13).** These are no longer the in-world backdrop: the world draws pixel parallax sets (W8, `world-sprites/09-parallax.md`). The paintings are realm key art for realm pages, store and social images and loading cards, built by `scripts/build-scene-art.py --only=bg-town-<realm>`, not by import-assets.py. P3 until a surface asks for them.

