# Realm town backdrops (menu layer, painterly)

Folder: `Assets/Locations/Towns/`. The painterly style block, the export specs and the save checklist are in [../README.md](../README.md). Paste **Prompt** as the positive prompt and **Negative** as the negative prompt; the spec line is the export contract.

Backdrops stay painterly and the world sprites stand in front of them. Keep the bottom fifth a calm, low-contrast ground plane with no strong edges; keep values a step darker and less saturated than the sprite palette so outlined sprites pop; leave no foreground objects a sprite would have to stand inside. Built by `scripts/build-scene-art.py --only=bg-town-<realm>`, not by import-assets.py.
